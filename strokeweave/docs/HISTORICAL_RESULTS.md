# Preserved results and claim boundaries

The prior bounded NIST campaign is closed and was not rerun or retuned for
this release. Its protocols, failures and frozen source remain in the private
research archive, unchanged. This file contains aggregate results, not data.

There were **18** raw floating-point output diagnostics, not eight: six
missed the locked precision criterion, spanning three datasets. All six were
rejected, with zero inaccurate outputs accepted. Four additional accurate
outputs were conservatively rejected. These are numerical precision and
conditioning findings, not six new software bugs or medical serious errors.

Five methods each completed nine tasks with five timing repetitions: 45
completions per method, not 45 independent samples. Direct deterministic,
shared strong gate, full VOI, TACTIC and actual Prefect each had zero wrong
accepts. All took the same exact-solve-plus-verification route. No method
avoided full solving. The geometric mean of the per-task median complete-job
TACTIC/direct ratio was **0.983445494**, missing the prospective **<=0.8**
target. Same-route timing variation is not algorithmic speedup.

Read-only timing decomposition found that import, process startup and
orchestration dominated these small tasks. The old direct path even imported
unused scientific libraries and computed an unused condition estimate. Any
removal of that work, persistent workers, caching, improved solver or tighter
certificate must also be available to direct, strong-gate and strong-VOI
comparators. The public numerical module does not publish the old greedy
policy, whose failed-exact-route edge case was found during audit but never
triggered the original results.

The portable release adds new generated-input implementation tests and
common-kernel cost checks. They neither replace this negative performance
result nor establish clinical reliability or an independent controller benefit.
Exact rank and source binding are now explicit public certificate preconditions.

## Publication assessment

The present contribution is a reproducible engineering prototype plus honest
reliability boundaries. Componentwise verified computing, fraction-free
elimination, caching and bounded recovery have established precedents. There
is no new visual-model architecture here. A Nature Communications methods or
application claim would still require a materially new general result or an
important independently validated scientific workflow benefit. No patient
execution, new training, cohort admission or label unsealing occurs here.

## Bounded next study (not executed by the release)

1. Identify a genuinely expensive scientific calculation with an independent
   task reference, input provenance and legal use, before seeing outcomes.
2. Lock eligibility, tolerances, failure strata, matched evidence and both
   cold/warm cost accounting, including proof preparation and all fallbacks.
3. Compare minimum deterministic verified flow, shared strong gate, strong VOI
   and same-policy mature orchestration. Accept ties or negative outcomes.
4. Report accepted-task accuracy, conservative refusal, complete throughput,
   recomputation and review burden. Do not infer human workload from call count.
5. Stop after the locked campaign. For stroke patient work, all existing
   patient-level admission and sealed-label gates remain mandatory.
